A Carb-Driven
  • Blood sugar spikes after carb-heavy meals
  • Fatigue or brain fog 1–2 hrs after eating
  • Strong carb or sugar cravings throughout the day
  • Belly / visceral weight gain
  • Elevated A1c, fasting glucose, or fasting insulin
  • Diagnosed with prediabetes, T2DM, or metabolic syndrome
  • High C-peptide or HOMA-IR above 2.0
  • Hunger 2–3 hrs after a full meal
  • Blood sugar "roller coaster" throughout the day
  • Family history of T2DM or obesity
B Cortisol-Driven
  • Wired-but-tired: exhausted but can't relax
  • Waking midnight–3am (alert or heart racing)
  • High fasting glucose despite a clean diet
  • Low morning energy; second wind at night
  • Salt cravings, especially in the morning
  • Poor stress recovery — takes days to bounce back
  • Unexplained anxiety or irritability
  • DHEA-S low, pregnenolone low, or flat cortisol curve
  • Chronic stress, burnout, or trauma history
  • Glucose elevated in the morning, lower by afternoon
C Inflammatory / Gut
  • Bloating or discomfort after most meals
  • Food sensitivities that have increased over time
  • Brain fog that worsens after eating
  • hs-CRP elevated (above 1.0 mg/L)
  • Ferritin elevated (>150 women / >200 men)
  • Joint pain or muscle aches without clear cause
  • Multiple antibiotic courses in your lifetime
  • Skin issues: eczema, psoriasis, acne, or rosacea
  • Gas, bloating, constipation, or loose stools
  • Omega-3 index low or never tested
D Thyroid / Hormonal
  • Cold hands and feet even in warm environments
  • Hair thinning — scalp, eyebrows, or body hair
  • Low libido or changes in sexual function
  • Weight gain despite no change in diet or exercise
  • Dry skin, brittle nails, or slow wound healing
  • Fatigue that doesn't improve with more sleep
  • Perimenopause or menopause symptoms (women)
  • Low testosterone or andropause symptoms (men)
  • TSH, Free T3, or Free T4 outside optimal range
  • SHBG high or sex hormones consistently imbalanced
E Mitochondrial
  • Deep, persistent fatigue — even after full sleep
  • Feel cold despite normal thyroid labs
  • History of toxin, chemical, or mold exposure
  • Symptoms started or worsened after a viral illness
  • Exercise intolerance — feel worse after working out
  • Sensitivity to chemicals, fragrances, or medications
  • Brain fog, memory lapses, or slowed processing
  • Widespread muscle pain or fibromyalgia diagnosis
  • Marked energy decline over the past 5–10 years
  • Family history of autoimmune or mitochondrial disease
