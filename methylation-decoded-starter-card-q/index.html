Choose One Lever

Example ranges to discuss — pick one, at the low end. Match the direction to your lean.

If leaning UNDER / high homocysteine — lean in

  • Methyl-B12  ~1,000–2,000 mcg
  • Methylfolate  ~400–1,000 mcg (or folinic acid if too stimulating)
  • Betaine (TMG)  ~1–3 g (backup route)
  • Choline / CDP-choline  ~250–500 mg
  • Riboflavin (B2) — especially if you carry MTHFR C677T
  • Creatine  3–5 g/day (spares methyl groups)
  • Protein  ~1.2–1.6 g/kg/day

If leaning OVER / slow-COMT / reactive — ease off

  • Gentler folinic acid instead of high-dose methylfolate
  • Niacinamide (B3) as a small-dose methyl buffer
  • Magnesium glycinate as the foundation
  • Low and slow — reintroduce at a fraction of the usual dose
Same nutrients, opposite strategy — the lean decides whether they're allies or accelerants.
Educational only — not medical advice. Start low, go slow, change one variable at a time. Discuss any supplement with your own physician. Dr. Yoshi is a physician mentor, not your treating doctor.
