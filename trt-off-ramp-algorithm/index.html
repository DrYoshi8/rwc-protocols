6-Month Decision Point
Accept the data. Make the mature call.
›

After 6 months of executing the protocol, the data will tell you which of three failure points you may have hit — or whether you're in the clear.

Failure Point 1 — Testicular Atrophy: Ultrasound volume <12mL or >50% reduction from baseline. Leydig cells replaced by fibrosis. Once fibrosis sets in, no protocol rebuilds dead tissue. If you had hCG throughout TRT, this risk was largely prevented.

Failure Point 2 — Pituitary Desensitization: LH/FSH remain flat at 12 weeks despite maxed support. GnRH receptor density may never fully recover after >10 years of suppression without HCG. The pituitary can't hear the signal anymore.

Failure Point 3 — Cortisol Ceiling: Chronically elevated cortisol that cannot be modified by lifestyle or intervention. HPA axis dominates HPG. No enclomiphene or HCG overcomes a nervous system locked in threat mode. Cortisol is the antithesis to testosterone.
If Total T remains below 350–400 at 6 months: You've hit your biological threshold. Accept TRT as your permanent physiological anchor. Optimize it. There is no moral victory in chronic hypogonadism. The goal was always an extraordinary life — not natural testosterone.
HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)
Start: 500 IU · 3x/week · SubQ
Reduce to 250 IU · 3x/week if E2 >50
Reduce further to 250 IU · 2x/week if still elevated
Mechanism: LH mimetic. Binds directly to Leydig cell LH receptors. Activates cAMP/PKA signaling cascade → testosterone synthesis from cholesterol. Keeps Leydig cells metabolically active, prevents apoptosis, maintains testicular volume and Sertoli function.

Why it matters: Without LH signal (zeroed out by TRT), Leydig cells down-regulate all steroidogenic machinery and undergo involution. Within 6–12 months without HCG: average 45% testicular volume loss. At 3 years: 60% loss. Some is irreversible.
WHEN TO USE: Should have been running throughout TRT. Start at bridge phase regardless. Monitor E2 at week 4 — HCG causes intra-testicular aromatization. Find minimum effective dose that maintains testicular fullness and libido without E2 spike above 50.
Enclomiphene Citrate
Start: 6.25 mg · Daily · Oral · Morning
Escalate: 12.5 mg/day if LH flat at 4 weeks
Mechanism: Active isomer SERM. Blocks estrogen receptors at hypothalamus and pituitary, removing the negative feedback that suppresses GnRH. Result: pituitary releases more LH and FSH. Works at axis Levels 2–3.

NOT Clomid: Clomid has a 38-hour half-life and accumulates. Enclomiphene is the clean active isomer only — short half-life, no accumulation, no visual side effects reported with Clomid.
WHEN TO USE: Start at Week 1 of bridge phase. Take every morning. If LH doesn't respond in 4 weeks, double to 12.5mg. Do not substitute Clomid. Do not exceed 12.5mg without lab justification — risk of GnRH receptor desensitization from over-stimulation.
Kisspeptin-10
Start: 100 mcg · Daily · SubQ
Escalate: 200 mcg/day if pituitary non-responsive at 8 weeks
Mechanism: Top-of-axis neuropeptide. Activates GnRH neurons in the hypothalamus directly → pulsatile GnRH release → LH + FSH from pituitary. Works at Level 1 of the HPTA axis. Enclomiphene works at Levels 2–3. Combined = synergistic coverage of the entire axis.

Hierarchy: Kisspeptin → hypothalamus GnRH → pituitary LH/FSH → Leydig cells → testosterone.
WHEN TO USE: Start alongside enclomiphene from Week 1. These two are always paired. At 8 weeks if LH/FSH still zero, escalate to 200mcg/day. Do NOT combine with GnRH agonist drugs simultaneously — risk of homologous desensitization (receptor internalization).
CJC-1295 (No DAC version only)
100 mcg · Every night · SubQ · Minimum 4 hours fasted
Mechanism: GHRH (Growth Hormone Releasing Hormone) analog. Stimulates the pituitary somatotrope cells to release GH in a pulsatile pattern. Prevents the GH axis crash that accompanies testosterone cessation (GH is synergistic with testosterone).

No DAC vs DAC: No DAC version clears in 24 hours — pulsatile, clean. With DAC version has 30-day half-life and accumulates → disrupts pulsatile biology → diminishing returns. Always use No DAC for this protocol.
WHEN TO USE: Add at start of taper phase (Week 5). Take every night at same time, minimum 4 hours fasted. Always stacked with Ipamorelin in the same injection. Without GH support during cessation: expect ~20lb lean mass loss.
Ipamorelin
100 mcg · Every night · SubQ · with CJC-1295 No DAC
Mechanism: GH secretagogue — acts on ghrelin receptors in the pituitary. Different receptor pathway than CJC-1295, making them synergistic. Together they produce significantly greater GH release than either alone. Preserves lean body mass, maintains metabolic rate, preserves insulin sensitivity during the testosterone void.
WHEN TO USE: Always stack with CJC-1295 No DAC. Same syringe, every night, same time. 4 hours fasted. Never split them apart or use one without the other in this protocol. Continue throughout the full 6-month restart period.
Anastrozole — AI (Aromatase Inhibitor) · Last Resort Only
Start: 0.125 mg · Every 72 hours · Oral
Max: 0.25 mg · Every 72 hours
Target E2: 25–40 pg/mL · Never crash to zero
Mechanism: Aromatase inhibitor. Blocks conversion of testosterone → estradiol. This is the nuclear option — it should only appear in this protocol after HCG reduction, zinc, DIM, diet, and insulin management have all been attempted and failed.

Warning signs you're overusing it: Needing it weekly = your testosterone dose is too high or your metabolic health is poor. Fix the root cause, not the symptom.
WHEN TO USE: Only if E2 confirmed >60 on SENSITIVE assay WITH symptoms: gynecomastia, severe water retention, erectile dysfunction, emotional volatility. Start at 0.125mg every 72 hours. Retest E2 in 2 weeks. Adjust toward 25–40 target. If requiring an AI (aromatase inhibitor) every week long-term → protocol reset required. Lower the testosterone dose — don't add more drugs.

Estrogen Management Hierarchy — Follow In Order

  • Step 1 — Titrate HCG: If E2 >60, HCG is likely the primary driver (intra-testicular aromatization). Drop from 500 IU 3x/week → 250 IU 3x/week. Wait 4 weeks. Retest. If still high → 250 IU 2x/week.
  • Step 2 — Lifestyle: Remove all processed carbs. Go full carnivore. High insulin = high aromatase = more estrogen. Walk 1 mile at 16-minute pace daily — activates skeletal muscle glucose disposal system. Fix insulin resistance at the root.
  • Step 3 — Supplement: Zinc 75–100mg/day + DIM 200–300mg/day + Boron 10mg/day. Give it a minimum of 4–6 weeks before evaluating impact. This alone resolves most E2 issues when diet is clean.
  • Step 4 — Last Resort — AI (Aromatase Inhibitor): Only if E2 confirmed >60 on sensitive assay WITH clinical symptoms. Anastrozole: 0.125–0.25mg every 72 hours maximum. Target: 25–40 pg/mL. Never crash E2 to zero.

The RoVive Approach

R
Roots — The Foundation Beneath the Protocol
What needs to be in place before any hormone work succeeds
›

No TRT off-ramp protocol survives a broken foundation. These are the non-negotiables the RoVive Method addresses before adding any pharmaceutical or nutraceutical layer.

Sleep Architecture
70% of daily testosterone is produced during deep sleep. If you are not hitting 7–9 hours with quality REM and deep sleep cycles, no protocol on earth compensates. Fix sleep before adding herbals or peptides. Tools: magnesium bisglycinate, L-theanine, dark room, 65°F bedroom, consistent wake time.
Nutritional Density
Testosterone synthesis requires cholesterol, saturated fat, zinc, magnesium, selenium, and vitamin D as raw inputs. A low-fat diet is a low-testosterone diet. Minimum: 0.8–1g protein per lb bodyweight. Saturated fat is not the enemy — insulin resistance is. Prioritize: eggs, red meat, organ meats, wild-caught fish, raw dairy.
Circadian Rhythm
Cortisol and testosterone are circadian hormones — they follow the same clock. Disrupted circadian rhythm = disrupted hormonal rhythm. Non-negotiable: morning sunlight (10 min within 30 min of waking), consistent sleep-wake cycle, no blue light 90 min before bed, no eating after 8pm.
Gut-Hormone Axis
Estrogens are excreted through the gut via conjugation. Poor microbiome diversity (specifically beta-glucuronidase overexpression) uncouples estrogen and re-circulates it into blood. Fix gut health: fermented foods, fiber, eliminate alcohol, consider targeted probiotics. High estrogen with good diet = look at gut first.
